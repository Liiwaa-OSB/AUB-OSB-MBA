Life as an MBA student

Growth That Extends Past the Room

From day one, you join a diverse, dynamic cohort spanning a wide range of professional backgrounds, and levels of experience. We foster a collaborative environment where perspectives are shared, challenged, and expanded, enriching every step of the academic and professional journey. Alongside your studies, we offer networking, workshops, clubs, centers, and curated events, backed by the career services that help you make the most of every opportunity.

Workshops

Building Beyond the Syllabus

We extend learning beyond the syllabus through zero-credit workshops in team building, communication, consulting, and more, designed to be practical, hands-on, and immediately applicable.

1

Team Building Workshop

A three-hour interactive session designed to help participants build trust, strengthen communication, get to know one another, and develop effective teamwork skills. It combines fun and engaging activities with meaningful learning, while providing an opportunity to connect with colleagues from different OSB graduate programs.

2

Communication Skills Workshop

A five-hour hands-on workshop designed to help graduate students strengthen their communication and presentation skills, enhance professionalism, and communicate effectively in a business context. It combines practical learning with opportunities to develop written, visual, and oral communication skills that support academic achievement, career growth, and leadership.

3

Career Development Workshop

A two-day interactive workshop designed to help MBA students explore consulting careers in the Middle East, identify suitable specializations, and strengthen their professional positioning and interview skills. It combines expert insights, psychometric guidance, CV and personal pitch development, and case-interview preparation to support students’ career readiness and preparation for consulting opportunities.
